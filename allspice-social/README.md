# Allspice Social

Connects a bot team to the site's own social accounts through the hosted Allspice
Social MCP server (`https://social-mcp.allspicelabs.com/mcp`): Pinterest,
Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and TikTok.

## Connecting

In the Allspice bots, turn on **Settings → Allspice connections → Let the team post to
social**. It connects with your Allspice sign-in, with no separate login. Then ask a bot to
connect a platform: it gives you a link to that platform's own consent screen.

## What bots can do with it

Read: which accounts are connected, Pinterest boards, scheduled and past posts, and post
statistics. Change: post now, schedule, edit or cancel a post that is not live yet, and take
a live post down where the platform allows it. Every change waits for your approval in the
chat before it runs.
