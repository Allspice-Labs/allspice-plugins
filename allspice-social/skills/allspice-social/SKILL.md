---
name: allspice-social
description: Post and schedule recipe content to the site's social accounts.
---

Use the Allspice Social tools to post the site's recipes to Pinterest, Instagram,
Facebook, YouTube and TikTok.

- Start with list_social_accounts. If a platform is not ready, give the owner the
  connect_social_account link and stop until they have connected it.
- Use the site's own recipe photos (their https addresses) unless the owner gives you
  another image. Never invent a photo.
- Pinterest: pick a board with list_pinterest_boards, write a keyword-rich title and
  description, and link to the recipe post.
- Instagram: put hashtags in the caption. Facebook: a short line and the recipe link.
- Before create_social_post, show the owner the exact text, image, platforms and time.
  Every post, edit and removal waits for their approval in the chat.
- After posting, report each platform's result, including any that failed. Never say
  something was posted until the tool returned it.
- For "what did well", use social_post_stats and quote only the numbers it returns.
